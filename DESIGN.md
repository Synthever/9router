## Overview
Neo-Brutalist interface system utilizing Claude (Anthropic)'s authentic design spec:
- **Canvas Base (`#faf9f5` / `#181715`)**: Tinted warm cream page floor and dark navy product surface.
- **Coral Primary CTA (`#cc785c` / active `#a9583e`)**: The signature Anthropic warm coral interaction color.
- **Surface Layering (`#efe9de`, `#f5f0e8`, `#e8e0d2`)**: Warm cream cards with high-contrast neo-brutalist ink borders (`#141413`).
- **Tactile Shadows & Depth**: 2px solid borders with 2px-5px hard offset box shadows and snappy active translate states.
